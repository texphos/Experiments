**Hearthday privacy policy**

**Summary:** Hearthday does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

**What the app stores, on your device only**
- Your busy times, kitchen temperature, starter settings and formulas.
- Your bakes: plans, rise check-ins, ratings and notes.
- Whether you have purchased Hearthday Pro.

This data is saved in the app's private storage and is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your device settings. We cannot see it.

**What we do not do**
- No accounts, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, no data sharing or sale.

**Notifications:** reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

**Purchases:** Apple processes in-app purchases. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

**Beta feedback (TestFlight only):** if you choose to share beta statistics, the app creates a file you can review before sending. It contains usage counts and timing errors, not your name or notes. Apple's TestFlight terms also apply.

**Deleting your data:** Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
