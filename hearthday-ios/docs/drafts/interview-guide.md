# DRAFT: E1 interview guide (not used yet)

> For owner review. About 30 minutes, voluntary, unpaid unless an incentive is approved. Ask for consent before any recording. Don't show the app until the last section: we're testing the problem, not pitching.

**Screener:** bakes sourdough 2 or more times a month · weekday job or fixed commitments · has an iPhone (for E2 and E3 follow-up).

## 1. Context (5 min)
- Walk me through your baking week. When did you last bake? What for?
- How do you decide when to start?

## 2. The last three bakes (15 min). Specific events only, no hypotheticals.
- For each: when did you feed, mix and bake? Did anything land at a bad time?
- Was the dough faster or slower than you expected? How did you find out?
- What did you do? Why that? What did it cost you (sleep, the bread, plans)?
- What did you use to decide: a recipe, an app, a calculator, a rule of thumb, asking someone?

## 3. Current tools (5 min)
- Which apps or sites have you tried for schedules? What made you stop or keep going?
- Have you paid for any baking app? What for, and how much?

## 4. Reaction (5 min; only now show the prototype check-in screen)
- In your last collision, would this have changed what you did? What's missing or wrong?
- If this cost $9.99 once, who would you imagine buying it? (Note hesitation; don't pressure.)

## Scoring (for E1)
- **Collision in the last 3 bakes:** yes / no
- **Bad outcome or sacrifice:** yes / no
- **Already solved by (tool or rule):** ______
- **Paid for a baking app before:** yes / no, and the price
- Note verbatim quotes. Keep interviewee identities separate from the notes.
