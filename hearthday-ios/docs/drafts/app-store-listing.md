**Description:**

Sourdough takes a day or two. Your week has work, sleep and plans. Hearthday fits one around the other.

PLAN AROUND YOUR WEEK
Tell Hearthday when you're usually busy and when you want bread. It works backward from your oven time, adjusting the feed ratio, starter amount and fridge proof so the hands-on steps land when you're free. If a time can't work, it says what's in the way and offers the earliest time that fits.

WHEN THE DOUGH DOESN'T FOLLOW THE RECIPE
Check the rise mid-bulk. Hearthday estimates when the dough is likely to be ready and suggests what to do: shape when you're free, fridge it before bed, or stay up. Each option shows its finish time and trade-off.

GETS TO KNOW YOUR KITCHEN
At shaping, tell Hearthday whether the dough was under, just right or over. Your "just right" bakes tune future estimates. Pro applies them to your plans.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your bakes stay on your iPhone.

HEARTHDAY PRO: ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Personal timing applied to plans, unlimited formulas and your full journal. Planning, check-ins and reminders are free. No subscription. Family Sharing supported.

Times are estimates based on temperature and your own bakes. Dough varies, so always judge readiness by the dough itself.
