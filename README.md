# Sugar Flap

A candy-land Flappy Bird. Guide a gumdrop through candy-cane gates, grab wrapped sweets for bonus points, and chase a new best as the sky melts from cotton candy to berry night.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8765`.

## Controls

- **Play**, then **Space**, **click**, or **tap** to flap. The gumdrop hovers until that first flap.
- **P** or **Esc** to pause
- **M** to mute

Passing a gate scores one point. Wrapped candies in the gap are worth an extra point. The course gets a little quicker as the score climbs. Your best is saved in this browser.
