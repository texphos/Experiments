Duty Packet

A municipal grant packet for a small-town police department. Packet and checklist only. Nothing is filed.

1. Agency

Guided fields only. Do not paste a finished sample town. Every number later in the packet must already sit in a sourced field.

Name the state to route a state JAG pass-through. Direct federal JAG dies under about $10,000. This app will not invent a universal JAG PDF.

2. Sourced population — Census ACS 5-year

This town only. Never ACS 1-year. Never a county number on the town. If the lookup fails, the field stays blank. CrimeGrade, City-Data, Wikipedia, and nearby cities are not used.

Population is blank until ACS 5-year returns a row.

3. Sourced crime — FBI CDE, this ORI only

If CDE has no row, leave blank. Do not fill from another agency.

Crime is blank until CDE returns a row for this ORI.

4. People who must sign

COPS needs two Authorized Representatives: the chief and the mayor. A clerk is not enough.

5. State JAG narrative frames

Plain civic language. Brackets stay until the sourced field exists. Edit only after the numbers are on the packet.

6. FY27 COPS Hiring readiness — not an FY26 application

The FY26 window is closed. This sheet is readiness only. No deadline is printed. COPS is not scraped.

7. Print

Filenames use agency, grant, and date so last year’s packet is not overwritten. Incomplete packets print with an incomplete banner and do not look finished.
